Ventic Pro V11.16.3

Customer accounts:
- Registration/login by mobile number.
- Existing orders automatically appear when customer registers with the same phone.
- 30-day customer session.
- Customer portal shows orders, invoices, balances and warranty.
- Customer invoice page supports browser Print / Save PDF.

Passwords:
- Minimum is now 6 characters for admin-created users and customer accounts.
- Seed validation is also 6 characters.

Accounting:
- New /admin/accounting section with permission accounting.view.
- Accepted Ventic Pro quotation automatically creates the invoice through the existing approval workflow.
- Approved extras and payments keep the same invoice synchronized.
- Accounting page self-heals older accepted quotations that have no invoice yet.
- The same invoice automatically appears in the linked customer account.

Database:
- Additive Prisma change only: Customer.passwordHash, Customer.active and CustomerSession.
